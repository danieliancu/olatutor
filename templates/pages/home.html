The rules we're building to

These hold whichever features come first.

“Unsure” is not “wrong”

Messy handwriting is never marked as a mistake. Unclear work goes back to be confirmed or reviewed.

Reads first, marks later

Reading never sees the expected answer, so it can't quietly “read” what it hoped to find.

The grown-up decides

Parents can overrule any mark. The original result stays on record beside their decision.

No ads, no open chat

No targeted advertising, no public profiles and no unrestricted chat for children.

Where we are today

The notebook has been tested in desktop browsers with simulated pen input. It hasn't yet been tried with a real stylus on a tablet, or by any child.

We don't collect children's data. That won't change until a full data protection assessment and legal review are done.
